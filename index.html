About our company!

Reboot© is an independent game and software studio headquartered in Germany, built on a singular passion: the intersection of creative storytelling and interactive technology. We opeRa at the frontier of indie game development, crafting experiences that prioritise depth, atmosphere, and originality over commercial formula.

Our primary focus is gaming. From pixel art adventures to narrative-driven titles that leave a mark long after the credits roll. We believe that the most powerful games don't need the biggest budgets; they need the boldest vision. At Reboot©, we live by that philosophy every single day.

Newest Project
DARK NIGHTS
In collaboration with Exoticly©

Dark Nights is a pixel RPG unlike anything you've played before. Set in a world draped in shadow and folklore, the game weaves a genuinely unsettling story through a cast of characters so bizarre, so oddly loveable, that you'll quote them long after you've closed the game.

The mechanics? Tight. Surprising. The kind that make you pause, grin, and immediately try them again. This isn't a AAA cinematic spectacle, it's something better: a handcrafted, weird, memorable pixel experience built with obsessive care by a small team that knows exactly what they're making.

Dark Nights is the result of a true creative collaboration between Reboot© and Exoticly© combining world-class pixel art with intricate game design that rewards curiosity. Coming soon.
